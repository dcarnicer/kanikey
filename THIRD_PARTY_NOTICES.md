## M5Unified and M5GFX

The firmware builds against [M5Unified and M5GFX](https://github.com/m5stack/M5Unified),
fetched by PlatformIO. They are MIT-licensed and are not vendored in this
repository.

## Noto Sans JP

The visual-pack generator can rasterize **Noto Sans JP** (SIL Open Font
License 1.1). The font is not distributed in this repository; the generated
glyph pack is personal and is not committed.

## WaniKani content

No WaniKani subjects, mnemonics, radical images or other account content are
distributed with this repository. The visual pack and caches are generated
locally with the user's own token and stay on the user's card.
