# KaniKey v0.2.0

The second firmware release for the M5Stack Cardputer ADV. It adds the reviews,
lessons, study, stats, audio and reminder work from round 2, a new 8 MB
partition table, and an experimental PaperDIY build. The public font pack is
published with the release for **Download pack** on the setup page.

**Hardware status.** Many round-2 features were checked on a real device on
2026-11-19 and 2026-11-20, but not all of their checks are finished. Each
bullet below says what is still open. Reminders, sounds and vocabulary audio
have not yet been checked by listening, and PaperDIY has only been built.

## Reviews

- All accepted meanings and readings are listed after an answer, with user
  synonyms and grouped readings. A hint line shows after a wrong answer.
- Typo policy: WaniKani's close-answer rule by default, or Strict.
- Review order can be set in Settings › Reviews (for example `Oldest available`).
- Wrong answers: `C` (Correct) and `A` (Accept the reading) actions, and a
  detail page for the wrong meaning or reading (`Tab` / `Esc` back).
- Optional: auto-advance, back-to-back parts and a minimal header
  (Settings › Reviews).
- Pause › **Wrap up** ends the session early.
- The summary lists your mistakes; `Tab` opens them.
- Typing: `Backspace` removes one kana or romaji unit, `Fn`/`Ctrl` +
  `Backspace` clears the answer, and answers are capped at 64 bytes.
- Submission policy: at session end, every 5 reviews, or immediately.
- Open: held-key repeat and the 64-byte cap were not run on hardware.

## Lessons

- Browse the whole lesson list, page through each card (meaning, reading,
  mnemonic, components) and run a batch quiz before the batch starts.
- One confirmed start sends the batch to WaniKani. Browsing and the quiz never
  start a lesson.
- Lesson order: WaniKani's order, radicals first, or interleaved types.
- A workload check warns before a start that would add too many items.
- Topic pages for radicals, kanji and vocabulary.
- Open: a radical-only lesson on a low-level account has not been run, and the
  Start 5 offline and already-started cases are not checked.

## Study and stats

- **Extra study** (`P`): recent mistakes, recent lessons, critical items and
  the current level. Practice never writes to WaniKani and works offline.
- Critical items (below 75% accuracy) are found on each full sync. Settings
  can move them to the front of a review.
- Stats screen (`T`): a 24-hour forecast, the SRS distribution, level progress
  and a seven-day local history with a streak.
- The Home screen shows the SRS bar and the next review time.
- The critical list joins Wi-Fi when its items are not cached on the card, and
  `Current level` practice finds started kanji (both were empty before).

## Audio

- Vocabulary clips are cached on the microSD card (50 MB, least recently
  played removed first) during online syncs.
- `R` on a lesson card plays the reading clip.
- **Autoplay audio** (Settings › Reminders & sound) plays it at the reading
  point. It is off by default.
- MP3 clips only. OGG clips are not played.
- Open: no one has listened to the clips yet, and the 100-cycle soak and the
  current draw are not measured.

## Reminders and sounds

- Reminders (off by default): wake on battery at a daily time or when a set
  number of reviews is due, and show `N reviews ready` for 30 seconds. They
  use the counts from the last sync and need no Wi-Fi.
- Quiet hours (22–08 or 23–07) suppress reminders.
- Short tones for answers, the end of a session and reminders, with Low,
  Medium and High volume (sounds are off by default).
- Open: tones and the reminder banner need listening checks, and the sleep
  current has not been measured.

## Settings

- An eight-section menu with a registry of every setting, a help overlay (`?`)
  and **Reset settings**, which keeps the token, Wi-Fi and cache.
- Wi-Fi can be changed on the device (Settings › Account › Wi-Fi): it scans
  2.4 GHz networks and takes a hidden network name.
- Sign out removes the token and all cached account data.

## Sync and speed

- A quick background sync runs at boot and wake and shows `sync n/m` on Home.
  The manual sync screen has seven rows, a progress bar and cancel with `Esc`.
- Review batches and subjects are prefetched, and one TLS session is reused for
  several requests.
- Light sleep when idle, woken by a key or the timer. Its power saving is not
  measured yet.
- Home refreshes the clock and the due count every 60 seconds.

## Setup and onboarding

- The visual pack is two files: a public font pack (`glyphs.kgp`, Noto Sans JP,
  no WaniKani content) and a personal radical pack (`radicals.kgp`).
- **Download pack** on the station page fetches the font pack from the release
  and checks its size and SHA-256.
- **Upload radical pack** on either setup page takes a radical pack from your
  phone.
- The on-device Wi-Fi list and the phone portal both join a 2.4 GHz network.
- The setup network key is new for each portal start. The station page is
  locked with a 6-digit code.

## Stability fixes

- Firmware updates follow GitHub's redirects, and a bad first boot rolls back.
  A version that fails twice is not offered again.
- A slow or cut WaniKani response is retried at most once; reviews are not sent
  twice.
- Truncated 32 KB responses are split into stage requests and saved.
- The subject cache is streamed to and from the microSD card, which has its own
  SPI bus.
- The outbox is written atomically, so a saved review is not left half-written.
- The release build logs at level 0, which saves about 38 KB of flash.

## Experimental: PaperDIY

- A `paperdiy` build target with a two-button, read-only UI on the e-paper
  panel, a sleep frame and a sync cadence.
- **Build only.** It has not run on PaperDIY hardware. It is not in this
  release's assets; build it from source with `pio run -e paperdiy`.

## Upgrading from v0.1.0

v0.1.0 cannot update over the air. Its firmware check cannot follow GitHub's
redirects, so **Firmware › Check now** fails. Flash v0.2.0 once over USB. Later
releases can then arrive over the air.

Settings, the Wi-Fi and the WaniKani token are kept by both USB paths (NVS is
not erased). Pending reviews and the cache are in LittleFS, so read the
options before you choose.

**Option A: USB, same partition table (no source checkout).** Download
`kanikey-cardputer-adv.bin` from this release and write it to the active slot:

```sh
python3 -m esptool --chip esp32s3 --port /dev/cu.usbmodemXXXX \
  write_flash 0x10000 kanikey-cardputer-adv.bin
```

The image is 1,665,120 bytes and fits the old 1.75 MiB slot. The device keeps
the v0.1.0 table, so LittleFS (the cache, the outbox, the journal and the
practice stats) stays as it is. Over-the-air updates keep working while the
image fits the slot. This path has not been run on hardware. Details in
[docs/setup.md](../setup.md) §7.2.

**Option B: move to the 8 MB table (recommended; needs a PlatformIO checkout of
this tag).** The 8 MB table gives two 3 MiB app slots and 1.94 MiB of LittleFS,
starting at 0x610000. Before you start:

1. Sync so the **outbox** is sent (`S` on Home, and check that `Outbox` shows
   no pending items). Anything still in the outbox is lost when LittleFS is
   erased.
2. Erase the new LittleFS region, then flash from `firmware/`:

   ```sh
   ~/.platformio/penv/bin/python ~/.platformio/packages/tool-esptoolpy/esptool.py \
     --chip esp32s3 --port /dev/cu.usbmodemXXXX erase_region 0x610000 0x1F0000
   pio run -e cardputer-adv -t upload --upload-port /dev/cu.usbmodemXXXX
   ```

The token, Wi-Fi and settings are kept. The LittleFS starts empty: the first
boot formats it, and the cache, the local streak and history, the practice stats
and any unsent outbox are gone. The next sync rebuilds the cache. Full steps and
the error you see if you skip the erase are in [docs/setup.md](../setup.md) §7.3.

## Known limitations

- **No Bluetooth keyboard.** The BLE work is a desktop spike; nothing pairs.
  The built-in keyboard is the only input.
- **Audio**: MP3 clips only, no OGG playback, no stroke order. Playback and
  sounds have not been checked by ear.
- **Japanese text** needs the font pack on the microSD card. Without it, the
  device shows `Pack missing` until the pack is downloaded or copied.
- **Setup pages** use plain HTTP on the local network. The station page is
  locked with a 6-digit code; set up on a network you trust.
- **Wi-Fi** must be 2.4 GHz with WPA2 or WPA3.
- **Reminders** only wake the device on battery; on USB power, no reminder can
  wake it. Reminder wakes use the last sync's counts.
- **Not yet run on hardware**: the 403 "missing permissions" check, the
  radical-only lesson on a low-level account, and the 15-minute idle close of
  the setup network.
- **Not supported**: phones, watches, other boards and a mobile app. KaniKey is
  an unofficial app and is not affiliated with WaniKani or Tofugu LLC.
